AYANEO Next 2 85W Drops Solved.

No_Tech-Dad28:02Play AYANEO Next 2 85W Drops Solved. on YouTube

The AYANEO Next 2 couldn't hold 85W plugged in during my review. A viewer's comment sent me back to the bench, and one small change made the difference.

Four games didn't finish at the 85W plugged-in tier in the original review: power dropped to 55W or disconnected. Other owners hit the same thing, and some shared what worked for them. So I updated everything, set up four power configurations and re-ran the test on my unit before a flight. Huge thanks to Squallnine for the testing, the recommendations, and for pushing me to look again.

Two things to be clear about: this was a quick replication, not a multi-hour soak. And the review's DNFs still stand, because that's the out-of-box experience. But if your Next 2 is dropping to 55W, there's an inexpensive change worth trying before anything else.
