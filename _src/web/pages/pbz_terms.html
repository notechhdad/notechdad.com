Your PC
It writes the settings you choose into the game's settings file, launches the game through Steam, captures frame timings with Intel PresentMon (which needs administrator rights) and closes the game afterwards. It backs up the settings file first and puts your own settings back after each session, and does the same for your saves if you turn on Start position lock. Recorded routes replay keyboard and mouse input while the game is in front. It doesn't modify the game's installed files or read or change the game's memory. Keep your own backups of anything important, your saves included.
