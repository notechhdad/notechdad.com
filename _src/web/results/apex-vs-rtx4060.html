Cinebench GPU: Cinebench 2024 won't run on the Radeon 8060S, and Cinebench 2026 crashed in AMD's HIP runtime three times out of three, so the Apex has no score. DaVinci Resolve: Studio 21 on both. The Apex crashed the whole system twice before three clean runs, then won the overall score. Llama 2 13B: the model doesn't fit in the RTX 4060's 8 GB of VRAM, and the Apex's 96 GB of unified memory has no such wall.

Noise at 50 cm: the Apex on the Frost Bay dock measured 61.0 dBA idle and 69.2 dBA under sustained load. The RTX setup measured 64.0 dBA under sustained load, and at idle it was at or below 48.7 dBA, too close to the room's 46.7 dBA floor to measure. The handheld and dock are the louder system at every state.
