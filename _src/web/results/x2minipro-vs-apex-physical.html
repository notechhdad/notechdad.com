Why is it called the X2 Mini Pro?

X is the large-screen branch of OneXPlayer's 3-in-1 idea, which started with the OneXPlayer 2 in 2023: 8.4 inches, detachable controllers and a keyboard. The X1 took it to 10.95 inches in 2024. 2 is the line's second generation, in 2026. Mini is the 8.8-inch size class, first used by the X1 Mini in June 2024. Pro has twice meant a better chip in the same body: OneXPlayer 2 to 2 Pro (6800U to 7840U, 2023) and X1 to X1 Pro (8840U to HX 370, 2024). This time it marks the platform: the X2 Mini is Intel, and the X2 Mini Pro is AMD.

Three wrinkles. The Pro went on sale on Indiegogo on 15 June, before the X2 and X2 Mini were unveiled on 23 June, and shipped in September. It was teased as the "X2 Mini" with the Ryzen AI Max+ 395 in April and gained "Pro" for the global launch by 28 May, after which "X2 Mini" went to the Intel Arc G3 Extreme model. And it was announced with the 395 (16 cores) but shipped with the 388 (8 cores), with the same 40-CU GPU and the same TDP. The OneXPlayer 3 isn't an X3: it succeeds the OneXPlayer 2 and launched beside the X2.
