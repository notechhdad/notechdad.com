At the same 55W, the Mini Pro keeps its CPU cooler but runs the same GPU hotter and louder in the Wukong loop. I don't know why yet, and I'm not claiming a cause before Part 2. Noise was measured at 7 cm with a room floor of 45.8 to 45.9 dBA. Idle readings were too close to that floor to count, so they're left out. Neither handheld reports a fan RPM, only a fan percentage in OneXConsole.
