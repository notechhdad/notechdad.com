Pass B is not like for like: each device runs its own settings. On the rig, the pass B 1% low is 37.6 fps against 77.9 in pass A, and 17% of the time is stutter. The slow frames are on the CPU side (CPU about 28 ms, GPU near 13 ms), not the RTX 5090. At 55W, the 8-core Ryzen AI Max+ 388 leads the 16-core 395 by 4% in pass A and 8% in pass B.

My pick for the OneXPlayer 3 is 40W, High, XeSS Balanced with no frame generation: 76.0 fps with a 53.8 fps 1% low. In the bonus runs (8 Nov, game build 26367565, PBZ Bench 1.4.0, high-quality hair off), the game's own recommended settings, mostly Low, ran 40% faster than High: 106.2 against 76.0 fps. My pick for the rig is pass A's settings: 103.9 fps at 4K with no stutter.
