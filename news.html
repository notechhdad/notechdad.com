How these are tracked

  • DDR5-6000 32GB kit (2×16GB). AKIBA PC Hotline! (Impress Watch) surveys memory prices at six Akihabara shops over about a week in the second half of each month. Each point is the lowest price it found for a DDR5-6000 16GB×2 desktop kit, tax included, and links to that month's report. The same survey runs every month, which keeps the months comparable. The lowest price is one shop's offer that week, so a single sale can move it. Source: AKIBA PC Hotline!.
  • GeForce RTX 5070, GeForce RTX 5060 Ti 16GB, Radeon RX 9070 XT and GeForce RTX 5090. BestValueGPU checks the lowest new price at Newegg every day and filters out one-off mislisted prices. Each point is that month's median of those daily lows. Its record of new prices starts in March 2026, and the November point covers only the days checked so far. The RTX 5090 has no new price for September. Source: BestValueGPU (GeForce RTX 5070, GeForce RTX 5060 Ti 16GB, Radeon RX 9070 XT, GeForce RTX 5090).
  • Samsung 990 PRO 2TB. AKIBA PC Hotline! surveys SSD prices at four to six Akihabara shops over about a week in the second half of each month. Each point is the lowest price it found for the Samsung 990 PRO 2TB without a heatsink (MZ-V9P2T0B/IT), tax included, and links to that month's report. Source: AKIBA PC Hotline!.

Topics I'm tracking

GPU prices

GPU prices are out to lunch

Graphics cards are selling far above their launch prices, and the memory that goes into them keeps getting more expensive.

  • RTX 5060 Ti 16GB$799.99 against $429+86%
  • RTX 5080$1,679.99 against $999+68%
  • RTX 5070$869.99 against $549+58%
  • RTX 5070 Ti$1,169.99 against $749+56%
  • RX 9070 XT$799.99 against $599+34%

Newegg prices on 3 Oct 2026 against launch MSRP, from one retailer on one day. Bars run from 0 to 100% over MSRP. Source: Digital Citizen, 4 Oct 2026.

  • 4 Nov

    Team Group's CEO said on the company's 2 Nov earnings call that the memory shortage will likely get worse through late 2027, with "no downward price trend whatsoever". He expects supply and demand to balance in the second half of 2028 at the earliest. Contract prices for 16GB DDR5 chips went from $8.70 in October 2025 to $48 in September 2026. Acer's CEO disagrees and expects prices to start falling in the second half of 2027. Tom's Hardware ↗

  • 3 Nov

    ReportBoard partners reportedly raise RTX 50 prices this month. Colorful's reported rises run from $22–30 on an RTX 5060 and $150 on an RTX 5070 Ti to $380–450 on an RTX 5080 and $330–550 on an RTX 5090D v2, and GALAX reportedly adds $30 to the RTX 5060. The report comes from Chinese sources, and NVIDIA hasn't confirmed it. Wccftech ↗

  • 30 Oct

    ReportAMD reportedly plans to raise the price of the GPU and GDDR6 memory bundles it sells to board partners in November, which would hit Radeon RX 9000 cards. The report comes from the supply-chain source ChannelGate, which says GDDR6 prices rose about 8–10% in Q3. Higher prices may take a few weeks to reach shelves, and AMD hasn't confirmed it. Wccftech ↗

  • 30 Oct

    RumorThe RTX 5080 has followed the RTX 5090 off NVIDIA's US marketplace, which now lists no 80- or 90-class cards. Unnamed rumors say a 24GB RTX 5080 is coming in 2027. NVIDIA hasn't commented. eTeknix ↗

  • 20 Oct

    ForecastTrendForce expects DRAM contract prices to rise about 20% this quarter. That's slower than the roughly 80% and 40% rises of the last two quarters, but prices are still climbing, and spot prices keep going up too. TrendForce ↗

  • 17 Oct

    ReportThe RTX 5090 has vanished from NVIDIA's own marketplace, and Micro Center now lists it as "Not Carried" rather than out of stock. NVIDIA's Jeff Fisher told Taiwanese media the card hasn't been discontinued, as reported by Notebookcheck. Third-party sellers on Amazon asked about $8,500, per Tom's Hardware. Notebookcheck ↗ The FPS Review ↗

  • 8 Oct

    ReportAMD has reportedly raised its suggested Radeon prices in China for this quarter. The RX 9070 goes up ¥700 to ¥6,299, and the RX 9070 XT stays at ¥6,999. AMD hasn't confirmed it. TweakTown ↗

  • 2 Oct

    TrendForce expects memory contract prices to rise again this quarter, by 10–15% for DRAM and 15–20% for NAND flash, with AI servers served first. PC makers are already cutting SSD sizes in mainstream models. Evertiq ↗

  • 31 Aug

    TechSpot's survey of 10 countries found GeForce prices up 19% in a month and Radeon up 11%. Cards averaged 28% over MSRP, not counting the RTX 5090, which sat 136% over its $1,999 MSRP. TechSpot ↗

DLSS

DLSS 4.5 is here, and DLSS 5 is getting lighter

  • 4 Nov

    DLSS 5 reaches Hogwarts Legacy on 5 Nov, under Neural Rendering in the game's options, for RTX 50 Series GPUs and laptops, RTX Spark PCs and GeForce NOW Ultimate. War Thunder gets DLSS 5 in a few weeks, NVIDIA says. Game Ready driver 621.14 supports it. NVIDIA ↗

  • 20 Oct

    Cyberpunk 2077's 22 Oct update adds DLSS 4.5 Dynamic Multi Frame Generation and moves the game's frame generation, Ray Reconstruction and Super Resolution to DLSS 4.5's second-generation models, NVIDIA says. Game Ready driver 621.01 supports it. NVIDIA ↗

  • 13 Oct

    NVIDIA says a new DLSS 5 model runs twice as fast, with about half the GPU cost of the September version and about 20% less VRAM at 4K. It claims up to 1.4X more frames in NBA 2K27. DLSS 5 is still RTX 50 and RTX Spark only, and reaches Hogwarts Legacy on 5 Nov. These are NVIDIA's own figures. NVIDIA ↗

  • 6 Oct

    Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 ships with DLSS 4.5: upscaling on any RTX card, frame generation on RTX 40, and dynamic multi frame generation up to 6X on RTX 50. Campaign early access starts 16 Oct, and Game Ready driver 617.42 supports it. NVIDIA ↗

  • 4 Sep

    DLSS 5 launched for RTX 50 cards only, and it costs a lot of frames. In NBA 2K27, TechSpot's RTX 5090 at 4K fell from 192 to 76 fps with it on, and an RTX 5070 at 1440p from 217 to 67 fps. NVIDIA says RTX 40 support comes later. TechSpot ↗

FSR 4

FSR 4.1 reaches its first handheld

  • 3 Nov

    Tom's Guide tested FSR 4 on the ROG Xbox Ally X20. At the same Balanced setting it ran no faster than FSR 3, and sometimes a little slower: God of War Ragnarök went from 39 to 38 fps. FSR 4 Performance beat FSR 3 Balanced, at 43 fps, and looked sharper. Tom's Guide ↗

  • 23 Oct

    ASUS has put a preview GPU driver with FSR 4.1 in Armoury Crate SE for the ROG Xbox Ally X and X20, under Update Center, then Preview Updates. ASUS warns preview updates may be buggy, and the driver rolls out to everyone once it leaves preview. The base ROG Xbox Ally's update is "coming soon", with no date. ASUS ROG ↗

  • 20 Oct

    FSR 4.1 now runs on Radeon RX 6000 cards and Ryzen AI Max chips with Adrenalin 26.10.1. A lighter model covers Ryzen AI 9 laptops and the Ryzen AI Z2 Extreme, Ryzen Z2 Extreme and Ryzen Z2 A handheld chips, but the ROG Xbox Ally X is the only handheld AMD names. Timing depends on each maker, and AMD doesn't mention the Steam Deck or Legion Go. AMD ↗

  • 20 Oct

    AMD and Xbox say FSR 4.1 is also coming to the Xbox Series X|S consoles, and Xbox's CTO says it will be tuned for the ROG Xbox Ally X and X20. There's no date yet. Pure Xbox ↗

  • 7 Oct

    AMD's Jack Huynh says official FSR 4 support reaches APUs, gaming laptops and handheld PCs before the end of 2026. A lighter version for RDNA 3-based APUs is in the works, which TechSpot says would cover Ryzen AI Max devices, most ROG Ally models and the Legion Go. There's no device list or date yet. TechSpot ↗

  • 1 Oct

    RumorLeaked specs for the Ryzen Z3 and Z3 Extreme claim Zen 6 cores and a 12-unit "RDNA 4m" GPU with hardware suited to FSR 4. AMD hasn't announced either chip. Guru3D ↗

  • Jun

    A lighter INT8 version of FSR 4 reached Radeon RX 7000 cards. RX 9000 cards have had it since launch. RDNA 2 devices like the Steam Deck and the base ROG Xbox Ally will likely wait until next year. TechSpot ↗

More news

  • 5 Nov 2026
    SteamOS

    An unofficial project brings XMX-accelerated XeSS to Proton

    An independent developer's experimental project, xess-xmx-linux, runs XeSS upscaling and XeSS Frame Generation on Intel's XMX matrix units in Proton games, instead of the slower DP4a path Proton uses today. It was built on a OneXPlayer 3 with the Arc G3 Extreme and also tested on an Arc B580. PC Gamer reports about 29% more frames in Cyberpunk from upscaling alone, about 145% with frame generation, and 16% in Forza Horizon 6. It isn't an Intel release; Intel's own Proton support is still pending.

    Source: PC Gamer

  • 5 Nov 2026
    Market

    Switch 2 sales fall 37%, and Nintendo plans no further price rise

    Nintendo sold 6.49 million Switch 2 consoles from April to September, down from 10.36 million a year earlier, and now expects 16.5 million for the year to March 2027. Reuters reports that Nintendo's president told investors the company can get enough RAM to build the Switch 2 and plans no further price increase. The price went from $449 to $499 this year.

    Sources: Notebookcheck, PC Gamer

  • 4 Nov 2026
    Driver

    NVIDIA Game Ready 621.14 is the new WHQL driver

    Game Ready 621.14 is ready for World of Warcraft: Forever and for DLSS 5 in Hogwarts Legacy, which starts on 5 Nov for RTX 50 Series and RTX Spark. It's the first WHQL driver since 621.01 and the 621.12 hotfix. My pick for stability is still Game Ready 617.42.

    Source: NVIDIA

  • 4 Nov 2026
    eGPU

    ASUS launches the ROG XG Station 3 Thunderbolt 5 eGPU dock

    The open-frame dock has two Thunderbolt 5 ports and two 10Gbps USB-C ports, and charges a laptop at up to 140W. It takes RTX 50, Radeon RX 9000 and Arc cards. Unlike the XG Station 2, it ships without a power supply, so you add your own SFX or SFX-L unit. ASUS hasn't announced a price or release date.

    Source: Notebookcheck

  • 3 Nov 2026
    Mini PC

    GMKtec teases a mini PC with OCuLink or two SSDs

    GMKtec's next mini PC is about the size of its Panther Lake EVO-T2S. It will come with either one SSD slot and an OCuLink port for an eGPU, or two SSD slots for up to 16TB, and GMKtec is asking fans to vote. The name, chip and price aren't confirmed.

    Source: Notebookcheck

  • 30 Oct 2026
    Driver

    AMD Adrenalin 26.10.2 is a fix-only optional driver

    AMD's 26.10.2 adds no new games. It fixes crashes and timeouts, including ones when opening overlays such as Xbox Game Bar and when using FSR Frame Generation on RX 9000 cards. It's an Optional release, so 26.10.1 stays AMD's Recommended driver, and AMD's notes say the package doesn't support handheld PCs.

    Source: AMD

  • 29 Oct 2026
    Software

    Winhanced 1.0 beta brings FSR 4.1 to AMD handhelds

    Winhanced is a free, controller-friendly launcher for Windows 11 handhelds and an alternative to Xbox's full screen experience. Version 1.0, a beta from 31 Oct, adds an in-game overlay with power modes and dynamic TDP, FSR 4.1 on AMD handhelds that AMD doesn't officially support yet, built-in OptiScaler (not in games with anti-cheat), game compression and GeForce NOW.

    Source: PC Guide

  • 27 Oct 2026
    Driver

    NVIDIA hotfix 621.12 fixes the 621.01 video freeze

    Hotfix driver 621.12 fixes the video freezes and black screens some RTX 50 and RTX Spark owners saw after updating to Game Ready driver 621.01. It also fixes an Unreal Engine 5.8 crash with Hit Lighting, HDMI corruption through a StarTech Thunderbolt 3 adapter on RTX Spark systems, and flicker on the MSI N16 Flip. NVIDIA will fold the fixes into its next WHQL driver, with no date given. Be aware that NVIDIA calls hotfix drivers beta and says they get a much shorter QA process. My pick for stability is still Game Ready 617.42.

    Sources: Club386, HotHardware

    ROG Xbox Ally X20 reviews: a better screen, the same speed
