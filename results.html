The most useful discovery: check CPU Boost. OneXConsole showed 40W while Turbo, which OneXPlayer says turns on CPU Boost, was off. After turning Turbo on, God of War III held a steady 30 fps in combat and passed save/load, and Gran Turismo 5 finished an Arcade race, bought a car and still had it after a reload. Emulator builds and game settings also changed along the way, so this isn't a controlled measurement of Turbo on its own. It does mean the Turbo state belongs in the test record next to the power limit.

God of War III settings for the passing run: Turbo on at 40W; PPU and SPU decoders LLVM; SPU threads Auto; SPU block size Mega; XFloat Approximate; Vulkan at 720p, 100%; shader precision Ultra; Write Color Buffers on; patches Disable MLAA v3.0 and Disable SPU MLAA v1.5. Dropping to 50% resolution made it look worse without helping.

Uncharted 3 and The Last of Us both look far better with the in-game MLAA disabled and GPU Lighting enabled, and both reach gameplay, but they kept freezing. Uncharted 3's 29.04 fps screenshot was captured at the moment it froze, which is why an FPS counter on its own proves nothing.

These are first results from the sections I tested, not full playthroughs, official RPCS3 compatibility ratings or a guide to Android performance. The test procedure carries over to the AYN Odin 3; the Windows settings don't.
